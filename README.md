# Interactive Sales Dashboard

Simulated dataset of 12 months of sales across 4 regions and 3 product categories, created for practice with AI assistance.

## Goal
Build an interactive dashboard that shows performance against target, trends over time, and where to act.

## Dashboard features
- Headline numbers: total revenue, total orders, average order value and % of target
- Line chart: monthly revenue trend
- Column chart: revenue vs target by region
- Pie chart: revenue share by category
- Slicers: filter everything by region and category

## Key metrics
- Total revenue: K1,703,930 from 6,526 orders
- Average order value: K261
- Overall: 96.9% of target, about K54,000 short

## Key findings
- Copperbelt was the only region above target (117%), and its revenue grew about 53% from the first half of the year to the second.
- Lusaka reached 93% of target, Eastern 85% and Southern 85%.
- Southern's sales stayed flat in the second half of the year, while every other region grew by 22% or more.
- Electronics brought in the most revenue (37%) from the fewest orders (1,393), at about K450 per order. Clothing had the most orders (3,187) but only about K179 per order.
- December was the strongest month (K204,830), about 24% higher than November. February was the weakest (K105,870).

## Recommendations to test
- Investigate why Southern's sales stopped growing, starting with its customers, stock and competition.
- Learn what is working in Copperbelt and try it in other regions.
- Plan extra stock and staff for December.
- Try bundles or add-ons to raise the value of clothing orders.

## Limitations
- The data is simulated, so results are for practice only.
- Only one year is included, so seasonal patterns can't be confirmed.
- Costs and profit are not included, only revenue.
- How the targets were set is unknown, so missing a target may reflect the target rather than performance.
